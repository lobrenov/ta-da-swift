# Privacy Policy

Effective 24 November 2026. This policy covers the Ta Da apps for Mac and iPhone.

## Summary
Ta Da collects no data. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it sends nothing about you or your files to the developer or anyone else.

## Your files
Ta Da reads and writes the markdown files in the folder you choose. They stay in that folder, on your device or in whatever sync service holds it (such as iCloud Drive). Ta Da keeps its settings and its access to that folder on your device.

## Calendars and reminders
If you allow it, Ta Da reads your calendar events and reminders to show them in the Agenda. It never changes them, and they never leave your device. You can turn access off in Settings at any time.

## Notifications
If you turn them on, Ta Da sets its app badge on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

## Updates (Mac, GitHub download only)
The Mac app downloaded from GitHub checks GitHub for updates. Like any web request, this shares your IP address and app version with GitHub, under [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). The App Store apps get updates from the App Store and make no such check.

## Children
Ta Da collects no data from anyone, including children.
