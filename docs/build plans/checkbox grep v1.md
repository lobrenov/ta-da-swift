# Checkbox grep v1

## Context
Ta Da is one file per task, project and area. Subtasks in notes (meeting minutes, daily notes) are counted but never listed outside their file. This plan lists open subtasks from notes where they belong, and lets any subtask line carry a due date (`>YYYY-MM-DD`) and a parent (`@[[Title]]`) inline, with the date menu and move menu opening as they're typed. It covers Ta Da on Mac and iPhone and the Obsidian plugin. Design: the canvas's Checkbox grep page.

## Decisions
- **Setting**: `editor.noteSubtasks` in `Ta Da/settings.json`, default off, shared with the plugin. Settings ▸ Editor gains a Subtasks section with "Subtasks from notes". Off turns off the listing only; the inline attributes, their drawing and their menus work either way.
- **Listed**: open boxes (`[ ] [/] [>] [<]`, the `.open` state) in notes (files with no `type`), daily and week notes included. Icon markers, code, quotes and callouts are skipped, as Extract to Task skips them. A nested box isn't listed on its own; its parent line shows the subtasks glyph.
- **Attributes**: on a checkbox line's first line, after whitespace. `>YYYY-MM-DD` is the due date; `@[[Title]]` is a parent, a project or area resolved by title as `project:` is. The first of each counts; an unresolved parent is drawn struck and doesn't place the line. Attributes work in every file: a project's own subtask with `>2026-10-09` also shows on that day.
- **Where a line lands**: a date puts it in the Agenda on that day (and its week); an open past date puts it in Today, overdue. A parent puts it on that project's or area's page. Both: both places. A note's line with neither goes to Loose tasks. A task's, project's or area's line without attributes stays only in its file.
- **Drawn as cards** under a "From notes" `CountGroupHeader`, after the page's own tasks: one card per source file (note icon, title, folder), its listed lines with tickable boxes, the date pill (Agenda: only when overdue, as task rows) and the parent chip (hidden on that parent's own page). Clicking a box splices its marker (`ChecklistEditor.click`); the line stays struck until the list reloads. Clicking a line's text opens the file at that line. Right-click is the checkbox menu.
- **Dots**: a dated line counts as a due task in `dayMarks`.
- **Editor drawing**: `>2026-10-02` draws as the date pill (accent tint, deadline when overdue), `@[[Ta Da MVP]]` as a chip (ring or cube, title in `link`). Off the caret's line only, as tags.
- **Typing**: on a checkbox line, `>` after whitespace opens the date menu at the caret; the typed text after it is the phrase (`DatePhrase.parse`; "fri" on a Friday is today); ↵ writes `>2026-10-02 `, esc leaves the text. `@` after whitespace opens the move menu (areas and projects, New project, no Loose tasks); ↵ writes `@[[Ta Da MVP]] `. The editor keeps the keys, as the tag menu does. Clicking a drawn pill or chip reopens its menu and replaces the attribute. iPhone: the keyboard bar lists the choices, as for tags; its `>` and `@` keys show on checkbox lines.
- **Renames**: `@[[Title]]` is a wiki link, so `relinkBodies` and backlinks already cover it.
- **Extract to Task** strips the attributes from the title and writes `>` as `dueDate` and `@` as `project` or `area` (over the host's parent).
- **Obsidian**: same rules. "From notes" cards in Ta Da: Agenda and in a project's or area's list in Ta Da: Tasks; Live Preview decorations as `linkBox`; `EditorSuggest`s for `>` and `@`; Settings tab row after "Task checkboxes on links".
- **Versions**: Ta Da 0.41.0, plugin 0.8.0 (tags `feature`).
- **Not in v1**: inheriting a parent from a note's heading or frontmatter; a deadline syntax; statuses other than ticking from a card; Extract on iPhone; iPad.

## Steps
1. **Core** (TaDaCore). `Checkbox` gains `text`, `due: LocalDate?` and `parent: String?` (from `MarkdownFacts.TaskItem.text`; an `InlineAttributes` parser with the rules above, skipping attributes inside code spans and links). `VaultIndex` queries: `noteSubtasks(on:today:)`, `noteSubtasks(from:through:)`, `noteSubtasks(parent:)`, `looseNoteSubtasks()`, returning `SubtaskCard { path, lines }`; `dayMarks` counts dated lines. `VaultSettings.noteSubtasks`. `SubtaskExtraction` strips attributes; `NewFile.task` takes `dueDate`. A line writer for an attribute: `SubtaskLine.setDue(_:in:line:)` / `setParent`, splicing only the token. `docs/file-format.md` (Subtasks, Settings), then conformance cases (read, queries, writes, settings) and the fixtures regenerated. Check: core tests, written first, for each rule above (nested, code, callouts, two dates, unresolved parent, CRLF); `swift test --package-path Packages/TaDaCore` passes.
   *Delegate: `swift-dev`. Runs in parallel with step 2.*
2. **Editor** (fork). Inline attribute nodes (`>date` and `@` before a wiki link) on task lines, gated by a configuration flag, styled as bubbles off the caret line with the resolver supplying the chip's kind; `SlashCommandState.Trigger` gains `.date` and `.parent`, opened only on a checkbox line after whitespace, with their query rules and `insert` replacing the typed token; a click callback for a drawn attribute. Spec cases in `spec/data`, `spec/grammar` and FORK.md. Tag `0.16.0-live.6`, bump the pin here. Check: fork tests for parsing (off task lines, in code, at line start as a quote), styling and triggers.
   *Delegate: `swift-dev` in `~/Projects/swift-markdown-engine-extended`. Runs in parallel with step 1.*
3. **Lists** (app, Mac and iPhone). `AppModel.noteSubtasks` and its lists, filled in `updateLists`/`updateAgenda` when the setting is on; `SubtaskCards` (Mac) in `AgendaDayList`, `AgendaWeekList`, `TaskListView`, `ProjectBrowserView`, `AreaBrowserView`; the iPhone cards in `PhoneAgendaPages` and `PhoneScreens`; tick through `edit(path)`, open at a line; the setting in `EditorSettingsView` and `PhoneOther`. Check: `TaDaTests` (`AgendaModelTests`, `NoteModelTests`) for placement, ticking and the setting off; design renders; by hand in SampleVault (`git diff SampleVault`).
   *Delegate: `swift-dev`. After step 1. Runs in parallel with steps 4 and 5.*
4. **Editor in the app** (Mac and iPhone). `LiveEditor` turns on the attributes, resolves chips from the index, handles `.date` (a `CalendarPicker` that leaves the keys with the editor, its phrase from the query) and `.parent` (`ProjectChoice` rows as a `PopupMenu`), and the pill/chip click; iPhone keyboard-bar lists and keys. `ExtractTask` passes the attributes. Check: `LiveEditorTests`, `PropertyPickerTests`; by hand typing `>fri` and `@ta` on Mac and in the iPhone simulator.
   *Delegate: `swift-dev`. After steps 1 and 2. Runs in parallel with steps 3 and 5; touches no list views.*
5. **Obsidian plugin**. Port the attribute parser, queries, settings key, extraction and line writer to `src/core/`, run against step 1's fixtures; cards in `listModel.ts` and `NavigatorView` (Agenda, project and area lists, Loose); a Live Preview `ViewPlugin` for pills and chips; `EditorSuggest`s for `>` (parsed date, Today, Tomorrow, Next week, Pick a date… opening `DateModal`) and `@`; the settings row. Check: `npm test` (fixtures included) and `npm run build` pass; by hand on desktop and iPhone.
   *Delegate: `typescript-dev` in `~/Projects/ta-da-obsidian`. After step 1. Runs in parallel with steps 3 and 4.*
6. **Review**. Both repos' diffs against this plan's decisions.
   *Delegate: `code-reviewer`, given the diffs and this file, not my conclusions.*
7. **Docs and version** (me). `docs/architecture.md` (Agenda, lists, editor, Obsidian), the design framework's In the app table, the canvas reference pages updated from the Checkbox grep page and that page deleted; Ta Da 0.41.0 with release notes, plugin 0.8.0, the pairing row in `docs/versioning.md`. Check: full `xcodebuild … test -skip-testing:TaDaUITests` passes; UI tests only with your go-ahead.

## Risks
- Scanning every note's boxes on each index change: the cards are built from `item.checkboxes` already in the index, not by rereading files; `LargeVaultTests` timings before and after.
- `>` is a quote marker at a line's start and common in prose: the trigger and the parser need the checkbox line and the whitespace before it.
- Step 4 waits on the fork pin; step 3 can ship behind the setting without it.
