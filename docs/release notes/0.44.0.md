## New
- Undo for task changes: completing, reopening, status, moving, dates, repeat, tags, properties and moving to Trash. Use Edit ▸ Undo or ⌘Z on the Mac, or shake on iPhone.
- An Undo toast after completing, moving or deleting, for a few seconds.
- Ta Da opens on the view and file you left it on.
- Shortcuts and Siri: Add Task, Complete Task, Open Task, Open Project or Area, and Show View (Agenda, Loose Tasks or Logbook). "Add a task in Ta Da", "Show my agenda in Ta Da" and "Open ‹project› in Ta Da" work without setup.
- Open tasks, projects and areas show in Spotlight.
- Handoff: the file open on one device opens on the other, when both have the same folder open.
- Mac: ⌘K completes or reopens the picked tasks.
- Mac: ⌘C on picked rows copies their [[links]].
- Mac: Help ▸ Ta Da Help.
- A privacy policy, linked from About.

## Changed
- The iPhone editor and file title follow your text size.
- With Reduce Motion on, movement animations fade or don't play.
- With Increase Contrast on, muted text, borders, fills and accents are stronger.
- In Source mode, a property change no longer clears typing undo.
