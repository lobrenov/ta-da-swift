## New
- Switch between vaults. Ta Da remembers every folder opened on this device, most recent first. On the Mac, click the folder line at the foot of the sidebar, press ⇧⌘O or use File ▸ Open Vault; on the iPhone, tap the vault card at the top of Other. Open Another Vault… adds a folder, and Remove from List (right-click on the Mac, swipe on the iPhone) forgets one and leaves its files where they are.

## Changed
- Recent files are kept for each vault.
- The iPhone's Other tab shows the open vault and whether Ta Da is watching it in a card at the top, in place of the line under Settings.
