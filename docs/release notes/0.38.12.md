## New
- Group by Tag in the Agenda, Loose tasks and a project: a task shows under each of its tags, then No tag, then Completed.
- Filter by No tag: the filter menu leads with No tag, to show the files without tags, alone or with picked tags.
- Search finds folders by name, and opens a folder's page from its result.
- On iPhone, tap a task's project or area in the Agenda, or an area's or project's group heading, to open it.
- Choose Folder… and Change Folder… on the Mac can make a new folder.

## Changed
- Search shows areas with their cube on the Mac, and lists them on iPhone.
- On iPhone, the More menu no longer has Search (pull down to search), and Move to Trash comes last. A project's More menu drops Open Project File, which its top bar has.
- The app badge keeps up with the day without Ta Da running.
- A bare -, + or * stays as typed until a space follows.

## Fixed
- On iPhone, the slash menu keeps one height while you type, so the page doesn't bounce.
