## New
- Give a subtask a due date or a project by typing on its line. On a checkbox line, type `>` for a due date (`>fri`, `>3d` or a date) or `@` for a project or area, and pick from the menu. The date shows as a pill and the project or area as a chip; click one to change or remove it. On the iPhone, the keyboard bar has `>` and `@` keys on a checkbox line.
- Subtasks from notes. Turn it on in Settings ▸ Editor and the open subtasks in your notes, such as meeting notes and daily notes, are listed as cards: in the Agenda on their due date (or in Today once overdue), on their project's or area's page, and otherwise in Loose tasks. Tick a subtask on its card, or click it to open the note at that line.

## Changed
- Extract to Task carries a subtask's `>` due date and `@` project or area into the new task.
